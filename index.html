Chapter I

Overview

NoveU is a place to upload, organize, and read novels — your own shelf, with progress tracking so you always pick up where you left off. It grew from a school project into something closer to a small social reading platform.

Chapter II

Features

Novels & chapters

Upload novels chapter by chapter, or import a full EPUB and have it split automatically.

Reading progress

Every novel remembers exactly where you stopped reading.

Search

Find novels by title, author, or genre across the whole library.

Reviews & ratings

Rate what you've read and see what's rising to the top.

Rankings

A running leaderboard of the best-reviewed titles on the shelf.

Accounts & profiles

Registration with email verification, password recovery, and personal profiles.
